- **VERIFIED** — externally verifiable or documented.
- **PORTFOLIO-RECORDED / STATED** — from Amber's portfolio records; not externally verified.
- **ILLUSTRATIVE** — a model or reconstruction.

Hard rules:
- Reconstructed visuals visibly say **"Reconstructed Demo."**
