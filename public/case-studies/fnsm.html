Case Study

Functional Neurology & Sleep Medicine

Professional Experience Operations, Digital Growth & Administrative Systems

Challenge

A newly opened self-pay specialty practice in Murray, Utah needed more than referrals — it needed a direct-to-patient acquisition channel and the operational systems to serve those patients.

Strategy

Connect the full digital ecosystem — website/SEO, business profiles, listings, reputation, content distribution and patient journeys — directly to operational workflows.

Implementation

Operations, patient communication, scheduling, referrals, diagnostic testing coordination, website/SEO work, business listings, reputation management, content distribution, workflow development and digital acquisition.

Results — Portfolio-Recorded, not externally verified analytics

200+

Profiles audited, corrected or optimized

2,000+

Distribution across groups, pages, directories and social channels

3–5 / day

Sustained inbound website calls

5,000+ / day

Recorded website visitors after the ecosystem was connected

Portfolio records also state the practice reached profitability in under six months. No other numbers are claimed for this case study.
